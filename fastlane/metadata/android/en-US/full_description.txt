DacVolumeFix automatically unlocks full hardware volume on USB DACs and 3.5mm adapters connected to Android without root.

When an external USB DAC (such as the Apple USB-C Headphone Adapter) is connected to an Android phone, Android initializes the DAC's internal hardware volume chip to a low default (-20 dB to -40 dB). The standard Android volume slider only scales the digital audio before output, leaving the physical DAC output quiet and underpowered.

DacVolumeFix solves this issue:
- Automatically detects connected USB Audio Class (UAC1/UAC2) DACs.
- Sends standard USB Audio Class SET_CUR commands directly to the hardware Feature Unit.
- Restores full 0 dB unattenuated output with maximum dynamic range.
- Allows continuous volume fine-tuning from -80 dB to 0 dB.
- Runs silently in the background without opening the app or interrupting active playback.
- Zero battery drain when idle (no background services or wakelocks).
- Lightweight: only 2.4 MB with zero native C++ bloat.
